Swiss 4000ers

All 48 peaks of 4000 m in Switzerland and on its borders: every SAC route, grades SAC / French, season, train time and days.

L/F easy · WS/PD moderate · ZS/AD fairly difficult · S/D difficult · SS/TD very difficult · AS/ED extremely difficult · EX/ABO exceptional. Rock grade in brackets, e.g. (4a).
"Train & bus only" removes cable cars and chairlifts (the Gornergrat and Jungfrau railways still count as trains). Approach times and days are recalculated.
Train times: fastest connection on a Saturday morning (SBB timetable, Nov 2026) to the valley station where the approach starts. Summer-only buses (Ferpècle, Fionnay, Mauvoisin, Fafleralp, Grimsel, Gruben) are estimates. Days and approach times are estimates.
